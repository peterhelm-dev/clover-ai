# Clover — ChatGPT Visual Prompts

How to use:
1. Open a **new ChatGPT chat** and paste the **Style Anchor** first. Keep every image for one folder in that same chat so the style stays consistent.
2. Paste the prompts one at a time. Save each result with the filename shown.
3. ChatGPT outputs 1024×1024, 1536×1024 (landscape) or 1024×1536 (portrait). Resize or crop to the final sizes listed here in Canva or Figma.
4. **Add headline text yourself in Canva or Figma**, not inside ChatGPT. Image models still misspell words, and you'll want to edit copy later anyway.
5. **App Store screenshots must show the real app.** Take real screenshots, then use the backgrounds in folder 02 behind them. Never send Apple AI-generated "fake UI".

Folder layout:
```
clover-visuals/
  01-brand/
  02-app-store/
  03-landing/
  04-product-hunt/
  05-clinic-kit/
  06-social/
```

## Style Anchor (paste first)

> You're my visual designer for **Clover**, an AI nutrition and mood companion. You speak or type what you ate, Clover logs it, and it quietly builds a kind, honest picture of how you eat and feel. Its personality: calm, warm, knowledgeable friend. Never clinical, never a fitness app, never judgmental.
>
> Apply this style to every image I ask for in this chat:
> - **Palette:** deep warm green #15803D (primary), soft sage #A7C4A0, warm cream #FAF6EE (backgrounds), oat #E9E1D2, clay accent #C9876B (use sparingly), ink #1F2A24 for dark details. No neon, no pure white, **no red**.
> - **Look:** soft editorial illustration with a slight paper-grain texture, gentle natural light, rounded organic shapes, generous negative space. Calm, uncluttered composition.
> - **Subjects:** real, whole food (bowls, bread, fruit, tea, leafy greens), hands, kitchen tables, morning light, small clover leaves as a recurring motif.
> - **People:** when shown, diverse, ordinary and relaxed, in everyday settings. Bodies are never the subject.
> - **Never include:** scales, measuring tapes, calorie numbers, stopwatches, gym equipment, before/after bodies, warning icons, red X marks, streak flames, or anything that suggests dieting, guilt or medical diagnosis.
> - **No text** in images unless I explicitly ask for it.
>
> Reply "Ready" and wait for my first request.

## Tips for keeping the set consistent

- If an image drifts in style, reply: *"Match the style anchor exactly: palette, paper grain, soft editorial illustration, no text."*
- To get variations: *"Give me 3 variations of this, keeping the composition but changing the food."*
- Once you pick a favorite hero, upload it back and say *"Use this image as the style reference for all remaining images."*
- Export the icon and logo at full resolution, then trace them to vector (Figma "Vectorize", or vectorizer.ai) before printing anything.
